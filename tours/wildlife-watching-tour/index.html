Wildlife Watching Tour

Dry forest by boat, rainforest animals on foot · Half day · 5.5 hours · Cañas, Upala & Bijagua, Costa Rica

About this tour

If you want to see animals in their natural habitat, this is your opportunity.

We start with a guided boat tour through calm waters in the middle of the tropical dry forest, then enjoy a delicious typical Costa Rican lunch.

After a 40-minute drive, the reptiles, mammals and birds of the Costa Rican rainforest are waiting for you.
