Four Elements Adventure Tour

Zip line, horseback riding, tubing, mud bath and hot springs · Full day · 7 hours · Rincón de la Vieja & Liberia, Guanacaste

About this tour

Enjoy four elements of nature in a single day.

Fly more than 120 feet above the Colorado River on a zip line, then ride a horse through the fields of Guanacaste to the starting point of the tubing, where fresh water from the springs of Rincón de la Vieja volcano carries us straight to the restaurant for a typical Costa Rican lunch.

We close the afternoon in the thermal area: a volcanic mud bath, sauna and hot springs to renew your skin and soul.

Make it your own
