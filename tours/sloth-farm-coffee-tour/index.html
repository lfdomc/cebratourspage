Sloth Farm & Coffee Tour

Sloths, a wood-fired lunch and a real coffee plantation · Half day · 4 hours · Bijagua & Upala, Costa Rica

About this tour

Meet sloths and other rainforest animals in their natural habitat with a local bilingual guide.

Then sit down to a typical Costa Rican lunch cooked in a traditional firewood kitchen.

We finish with a guided coffee tour through a real plantation, following the coffee and cocoa process all the way to packaging and distribution, with tastings along the way.
