Insane Adventure: ATV & Rafting

1.5 hours of ATVs plus class III–IV white-water rafting · Half day · 5 hours · Corobicí River, Cañas, Guanacaste

About this tour

If you love extreme adventure combined with beautiful landscapes, this is the tour for you.

We ride ATVs for 1.5 hours across small rivers and towns of Guanacaste, then travel in a deluxe van to the Corobicí River for a class III and IV rafting adventure.

And of course, a typical Costa Rican lunch gives us all the energy we need.
