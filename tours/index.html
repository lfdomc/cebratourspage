Tours in Guanacaste, Costa Rica

Every tour includes ground transportation, a bilingual guide, entrance fees, a typical Costa Rican lunch and bottled water.

Our tours in brief

  1. Sloth Farm & Coffee Tour – Half day · 4 hours · Bijagua & Upala, Costa Rica. See sloths and rainforest wildlife up close, enjoy a lunch cooked in a traditional firewood kitchen and finish with a guided coffee and cocoa tour on a real plantation.
  2. Palo Verde National Park Boat Tour – Half day · 4 hours · Palo Verde National Park, Guanacaste. Sail the Tempisque River through Guanacaste's most important wetland looking for crocodiles, birds and monkeys, then enjoy a traditional lunch and visit the Guaitil pottery artisans.
  3. Wildlife Watching Tour – Half day · 5.5 hours · Cañas, Upala & Bijagua, Costa Rica. Two ecosystems in one day: a calm boat ride through the tropical dry forest, a typical Costa Rican lunch and a rainforest walk to spot reptiles, mammals and birds.
  4. Four Elements Adventure Tour – Full day · 7 hours · Rincón de la Vieja & Liberia, Guanacaste. Zip line 120 ft above the Colorado River, ride horses through Guanacaste, float down the river on tubes and relax in volcanic mud, sauna and hot springs at Rincón de la Vieja.
  5. Insane Adventure: ATV & Rafting – Half day · 5 hours · Corobicí River, Cañas, Guanacaste. For adrenaline lovers: 1.5 hours of ATVs across rivers and small towns of Guanacaste, followed by class III–IV white-water rafting on the Corobicí River.

Make it your own: All our tours and services can be customized. Choose your pick-up time and place, go private or with a group, combine activities or adjust the pace for kids and seniors. Tell us what you have in mind and we will tailor the experience for you.

Palo Verde National Park Boat Tour

Half day · 4 hours · Palo Verde National Park, Guanacaste

Sail the Tempisque River through Palo Verde National Park, the most important wetland in Guanacaste province.

Look for mangroves, local and migratory waterbirds, reptiles and mammals of the tropical dry forest of Costa Rica.

A Guanacaste-style lunch, the way our grandmothers cook it, is served around the stove. Our last stop is the indigenous community of Guaitil, where artisans show the process of making handmade clay pottery.

Four Elements Adventure Tour

Full day · 7 hours · Rincón de la Vieja & Liberia, Guanacaste

Enjoy four elements of nature in a single day.

Fly more than 120 feet above the Colorado River on a zip line, then ride a horse through the fields of Guanacaste to the starting point of the tubing, where fresh water from the springs of Rincón de la Vieja volcano carries us straight to the restaurant for a typical Costa Rican lunch.

We close the afternoon in the thermal area: a volcanic mud bath, sauna and hot springs to renew your skin and soul.
