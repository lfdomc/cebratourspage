Palo Verde National Park Boat Tour

Wetlands, crocodiles and birds on the Tempisque River · Half day · 4 hours · Palo Verde National Park, Guanacaste

About this tour

Sail the Tempisque River through Palo Verde National Park, the most important wetland in Guanacaste province.

Look for mangroves, local and migratory waterbirds, reptiles and mammals of the tropical dry forest of Costa Rica.

A Guanacaste-style lunch, the way our grandmothers cook it, is served around the stove. Our last stop is the indigenous community of Guaitil, where artisans show the process of making handmade clay pottery.
